A quiet cue when you lose track of time

LumenFocus silently dims your displays at the interval you choose. Take a break, stretch, or get a drink. Press any key to return immediately, or let the screen brighten automatically when the break timer ends.

Where is the app?

Find the small progress ring in the menu bar. Click it for reminder controls, or right-click for the native menu. LumenFocus does not keep a Dock window open.

Set up reminders

Choose a reminder interval and break length in Settings, then turn reminders on. Automatic, silent dimming is the only reminder style. You do not need to accept a notification first.

Return to your work

Press any key, including Escape, or click Back to work. The overlay closes immediately and a new full reminder interval starts. The dismissal key is not sent or replayed to another app. Your videos, calls, downloads and other app tasks keep running underneath the overlay.

Skip, pause, or stop?

Skip starts a new full interval. Pause prevents dimming for 1, 2 or 4 hours, or a custom duration of 1–1440 minutes. When the pause ends, timing starts again from a full interval. Stop turns reminders and work timing off until you turn them on again, even after relaunch. A saved pause also survives relaunch.

Does it detect meetings or videos?

No. Reminders do not depend on the foreground app, meetings, videos or input inactivity. Use Pause when you do not want a reminder for a while. macOS lock and sleep are handled automatically: work timing stops and overlays are removed while your Mac is unavailable.

Records and privacy

Records offers 7-, 30-, and 90-day ranges with CSV export. These are local timer records, not a goal or a health score. A missing date is not a measured zero. No account is required, and the app does not upload these records.

Need help?

Email yveluo@outlook.com with the app version from Settings, your macOS version, and the steps that reproduce the problem. Mention multiple displays if relevant. Screenshots are optional. The app does not attach or send records automatically.

专注忘了时间，让屏幕提醒一下

LumenFocus 会按你设定的间隔，无声地让屏幕缓缓变暗。可以休息、伸展，或喝点水；按任意键即可立即返回，也可以等到休息计时结束后自动渐亮。

在哪里打开？

在屏幕顶部菜单栏找到进度圆环。点击查看提醒控制，右键可打开原生菜单。应用不会常驻 Dock 窗口。

设置提醒

在设置中选择提醒间隔和休息时长，然后开启提醒。自动、无声渐暗是唯一提醒方式，不需要先接受通知。

如何返回工作？

按任意键（包括 Esc）或点击“返回工作”，遮罩会立即关闭，并重新计算完整提醒间隔。触发退出的按键不会转发或重放到其他应用。遮罩下的视频、会议、下载和其他应用任务照常运行。

跳过、暂停和停止有什么区别？

跳过会从当前时刻重新计算完整间隔。暂停会在 1、2、4 小时或自定义的 1–1440 分钟内不渐暗，到期后重新计算完整间隔。停止会关闭提醒和工作计时，重新打开应用也不会擅自开启。已设定的暂停截止时间同样会跨重启保留。

会识别会议或视频吗？

不会。提醒不依据前台应用、会议、视频或无操作状态改变。需要一段时间不提醒时，可主动暂停。锁屏和睡眠属于系统生命周期，会自动暂停工作计时并移除遮罩。

记录与隐私

记录页提供最近 7、30、90 天查看与 CSV 导出。这些只是本机计时记录，不是完成目标或健康评分；缺失日期不等于真实零记录。无需账号，应用不会上传这些记录。

需要帮助？

请联系 yveluo@outlook.com，附上设置中的应用版本、macOS 版本和复现步骤。如使用多显示器，也请说明。截图可选；应用不会自动附加或发送任何记录。
