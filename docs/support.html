Make room for screen breaks

LumenFocus is a local macOS menu-bar reminder. It records timed work and completed breaks; it does not measure eye health or actual screen exposure.

Where is the app?

Look for the small progress ring in the menu bar at the top of your screen. The app does not keep a Dock window open. Click the ring for the next reminder and controls, or right-click for the native menu.

Start and end a break

Open Settings → Rhythm and choose a work interval and break length. “Rest now” starts immediately. Every rest display has End break and Remind me later controls; Escape ends the break early. Early endings are not recorded as completed breaks.

Delay or pause?

Delay moves this reminder later. Pause suppresses reminders until the time shown while work timing continues. Starting a break while paused ends that pause. Resume does not override a foreground-app rule.

Why did the reminder wait?

Settings → Do Not Disturb controls foreground-app and inactivity rules. An app rule checks which app is in front; it does not detect a real call or screen-sharing session. Locking or sleeping pauses work timing. No keyboard or mouse input only changes the reminder style.

Records and privacy

Statistics offers 7-, 30-, and 90-day ranges. CSV export contains recorded dates in the selected range; a missing date is not a measured zero. Records remain on your Mac. Exported files are yours to manage.

Need help?

Email yveluo@outlook.com with the app version (Settings → Data & support), macOS version, and the steps that reproduce the problem. Mention multiple displays if relevant. Screenshots are optional; remove private content before sharing. The app does not attach or send records automatically.

给工作留一点离屏时间

LumenFocus 是一款本地运行的 macOS 菜单栏休息提醒工具。统计记录的是计时工作和完成休息，不代表实际用眼时长或健康效果。

在哪里打开？

在屏幕顶部菜单栏找到进度圆环。应用不会常驻 Dock 窗口。点击圆环查看下次提醒和操作，右键可打开原生菜单。

开始和结束休息

在“设置 → 节奏”选择工作间隔和休息时长，点击“现在休息”立即开始。每块休息屏幕都保留结束与延后按钮，也可按 Esc 提前结束；提前结束不计为完成休息。

延后和暂停有什么区别？

延后只调整本次提醒。暂停会抑制提醒直到显示的时间，工作时长继续累计。暂停期间主动开始休息会结束暂停；恢复提醒也不会覆盖仍然生效的前台应用规则。

为什么提醒暂缓了？

“设置 → 勿扰规则”控制前台应用和无操作规则。应用规则只判断哪个应用在前台，不代表检测到了真实通话或共享屏幕。锁屏或睡眠暂停工作计时；没有键鼠输入只会降低提醒强度。

记录与隐私

统计支持 7、30、90 天范围。CSV 只导出所选范围内有记录的日期，无记录并不等于当天没有工作。数据保存在这台 Mac 上，导出文件由你管理。

需要帮助？

请联系 yveluo@outlook.com，附上应用版本（设置 → 数据与支持）、macOS 版本和复现步骤。如使用多显示器，也请说明。截图可选，分享前请移除私人内容。应用不会自动附加或发送任何记录。
